Who We Are

Dependable Steel Distribution From Yard to Site

Yard Standards

Infrastructure & Quality Controls

How we protect material integrity from mill receipt to site unloading.

Covered Bay Segregation

TMT bundles, annealed binding wire coils, and structural sections are stacked off-ground by grade and nominal diameter to prevent moisture corrosion.

Weighbridge Accuracy

Every outgoing vehicle is weighed empty and loaded with printed weighbridge slips, cross-checked against standard IS 1786 unit weights (D² / 162).

Batch Traceability

Bundle tags, heat numbers, and Mill Test Certificates (MTC) or Batch Test Reports are archived and issued with project consignments upon request.
