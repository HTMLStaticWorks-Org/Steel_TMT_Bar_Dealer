How Price Enquiry Works

Four straightforward steps from Bar Bending Schedule submission to site delivery.

01

Share Requirement

Select steel brand, IS 1786 grade, diameters, tonnage, and delivery city — or upload your BBS list.

02

Stock & Rate Check

Our desk verifies yard availability across requested diameters and calculates daily rate + transport.

03

Itemized Quote

Receive a clear proforma quote with per-tonne/kg breakdown, GST details, and estimated dispatch window.

04

Verified Dispatch

Upon confirmation, steel is weighed, loaded with batch test sheets, and dispatched to your site.
