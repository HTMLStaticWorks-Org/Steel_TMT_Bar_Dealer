IS 1786 Grade Guide

Product Grade Information

Understand yield stress, tensile ratios, and ductility differences across Fe 415 to Fe 600 before submitting your Bill of Quantities.

Fe 415 14.5% Elongation

Fe 415 Reinforcement

Minimum 415 N/mm² proof stress and 485 N/mm² ultimate tensile strength (TS/YS ≥ 1.10). Reliable ductility for low-rise housing and boundary slabs.

Enquire Fe 415
Fe 500 12.0% Elongation

Fe 500 Reinforcement

Minimum 500 N/mm² yield stress and 545 N/mm² UTS (TS/YS ≥ 1.08). Balances steel tonnage optimization with strong frame rigidity.

Enquire Fe 500
Fe 500D 16.0% Elongation

Fe 500D Seismic Grade

500 N/mm² yield stress with higher 565 N/mm² UTS, 16% minimum elongation, and 5% total elongation at maximum force (Agt) for seismic zones.

Enquire Fe 500D
Fe 550 10.0% Elongation

Fe 550 High-Load Grade

550 N/mm² proof stress and 585 N/mm² minimum tensile strength (TS/YS ≥ 1.06). Engineered for deep foundations, bridges, and industrial bays.

Enquire Fe 550
Fe 550D 14.5% Elongation

Fe 550D Ductile High-Load

550 N/mm² proof stress paired with 600 N/mm² UTS and 14.5% elongation (Agt ≥ 5.0%), preferred in high-rise cores and infrastructure spans.

Enquire Fe 550D
Fe 600 660 N/mm² UTS

Fe 600 Ultra-High Strength

600 N/mm² yield stress and 660 N/mm² minimum UTS (TS/YS ≥ 1.06) to reduce rebar congestion in heavily loaded structural columns.

Enquire Fe 600
