Execution Process

How Your Steel Order Moves

01

BOQ & Grade Intake

Submit required brands, grades (Fe 415–Fe 600), diameters, and site location via Price Enquiry.

02

Yard Allocation

Stock is reserved from covered bays and bundled by diameter or cut-and-bend stirrup specification.

03

Weighbridge & MTC

Tare/gross weighment slips and matching heat/batch certificates are compiled prior to gate exit.

04

On-Time Delivery

Vehicle details and ETA are shared with your site engineer for smooth crane or manual unloading.
